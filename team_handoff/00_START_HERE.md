# SolMate Team Handoff

## Working title

**SolMate: ASU Compute Concierge**

Working tagline: **From research intent to a safer Slurm submission.**

## What this package is

This is a teammate-facing package for parallel work on product design, UI, marketing, video, pitch development, and presentation assembly. It contains no credentials and no private job data.

## The one-sentence idea

SolMate uses ASU AIR-hosted models plus deterministic validation to gather complete compute requirements, create an explainable Slurm script, guide a user through testing and submission on Sol, and diagnose failures using evidence from job logs and metadata.

## Start with these files

1. `01_PROJECT_BRIEF.md` - objective, users, workflow, scope, and architecture.
2. `02_BUSINESS_REVIEW_PITCH_COPY.md` - slide-by-slide pitch content for the included Business Review template.
3. `03_PROS_CONS_AND_POSITIONING.md` - strengths, weaknesses, objections, and marketing direction.
4. `04_TEAM_WORKSTREAMS.md` - parallel assignments and shared definitions of done.
5. `05_DEMO_STORYBOARD.md` - the product demo sequence and evidence to capture.
6. `06_VIDEO_SCRIPT_90_SECONDS.md` - concise submission video script with light humor.
7. `07_FINALIST_SCRIPT_4_MINUTES.md` - longer jury presentation script.
8. `08_SOURCES_AND_CLAIMS.md` - approved claims, caveats, and source links.
9. `plan_live_air_compute_concierge_20260902.md` - full technical implementation plan.
10. `Business_Review_Template_Unchanged.pptx` - retained presentation template to be populated from the pitch-copy document.

## Important pitch-format warning

The kickoff material requests a 90-second prerecorded pitch. Other slides describe finalist presentations as either five minutes or four minutes, and one section appears tailored to a data-science tutor use case. Confirm the active finalist format with event staff before locking the final deck or narration.

## Non-negotiable language

- Say **proposed script**, not guaranteed fix.
- Say **validated against documented constraints**, not proven on every Sol configuration.
- Say **AIR-hosted models**, and visibly show live model provenance in the demo.
- Say **the user reviews and runs the command**, not that SolMate autonomously submits jobs.
- Describe synthetic tests as fixture coverage, not universal accuracy.
